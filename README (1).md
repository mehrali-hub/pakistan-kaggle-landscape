This notebook presents the **first comprehensive analysis of Pakistan's data science community on Kaggle**. With over **6,000+ Pakistani Kagglers** actively contributing to the global AI ecosystem, Pakistan is emerging as a significant talent hub in the data science world.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- 🇵🇰 Pakistan has **6,057+ active Kagglers** — a vibrant and rapidly growing community
- 📈 **65% annual growth rate** — fastest growing among major countries analyzed
- 🎓 **45% are Bachelor's students** — driven by strong academic participation
- 🏙️ **Karachi, Lahore & Islamabad** are the top 3 AI hubs in Pakistan
- 🤖 **Machine Learning** is the #1 specialization (30% of community)
- 🏆 Users with **10+ notebooks** have 3x higher tier progression rate
